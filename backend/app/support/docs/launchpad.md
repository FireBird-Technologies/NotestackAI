# What the Launchpad does
A calendar and agenda for your posts, with auto posting to X, LinkedIn and Bluesky, email reminders, tracked links and engagement.

# Schedule a post
1. Open **Launchpad**.
2. Click **New post**.
3. Write the post, choose the platform and the date and time.
4. Save it. It appears on the calendar.

# Connect accounts
Use the connect chips on the Launchpad. Bluesky connects with an app password. X and LinkedIn connect through their own sign in. Connecting a platform is needed for automatic posting.

# Substack Notes and unconnected platforms
Substack Notes has no posting API, so those posts, and any post for a platform you have not connected, arrive as an email reminder at the scheduled time instead of posting automatically.

# Links and engagement
Links in posts can be tracked, and engagement is shown on the Launchpad.
