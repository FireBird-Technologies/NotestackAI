# Where it is
**Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Sections: Profile, What the assistant should know, Privacy and email, Plan and usage, and Account.

# Privacy and email
Control product emails and privacy options. **Allow public report links** decides whether reports and infographics can be shared by link; turn it off and shared links stop working. Every update email has an unsubscribe link.

# Plan and usage
See your plan and how much of each limit you have used (including reports and infographics), and manage billing.

# Account
Sign out everywhere, which revokes all your sessions, or delete your account.

# Signing in
Sign in with Google, or with your email and a 6 digit code sent to you. Codes expire after 10 minutes. If a code does not arrive, check spam and request a new one.
