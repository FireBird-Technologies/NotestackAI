# Where it is
**Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Sections: Profile, What the assistant should know, Privacy and email, Plan and usage, and Account.

# Privacy and email
Control product emails and privacy options. **Allow public report links** decides whether reports and infographics can be shared by link; turn it off and shared links stop working. Every update email has an unsubscribe link.

# Plan and usage
See your plan and how much of each limit you have used (including reports and infographics), and manage billing.

# Account
Sign out everywhere, which revokes all your sessions, or delete your account. Deleted from the iPhone app, the account and everything in it are permanently deleted at once. Deleted on the web or from the Android app, the account is closed at once and its content is permanently deleted within 30 days.

# Using the iPhone app without an account
On iPhone you can tap **Continue as a guest**. Guests get a small free allowance once per iPhone; it does not renew. After that, create a free account (your work comes with you) or subscribe without one. Connecting other apps and social accounts needs an account.

# Signing in
Sign in with Google, or with your email and a 6 digit code sent to you. Codes expire after 10 minutes. If a code does not arrive, check spam and request a new one.
