# Make an infographic
1. Open a notebook and click **Infographic** in the **Create** panel.
2. Under **Made from**, pick posts and/or chats (at least one).
3. Optionally describe **What should it show?**
4. Choose a **Layout** for the AI to build the page around: Auto (it chooses from your content), Flow chart, Timeline, Comparison, Hierarchy or Overview.
5. Choose a **Look**: Midnight (black and blue), Orbit (white with black type, blue orbit rings, a ringed planet and shooting stars).
6. Click **Generate Infographic**.
The AI designs the page itself from your material: it picks and arranges flow charts, timelines, hierarchies, comparisons, cards and numbers to suit the content, so no two infographics are laid out the same way. It runs in the background and the panel shows its progress. The card shows a preview. Click **Open** to see the full page, with the prompt and the sources it was made from. You can also add an infographic inside an interactive report.

# Landscape and portrait
Every infographic has two shapes. It opens in **Landscape** by default; use the **Landscape** and **Portrait** switch at the top of the viewer to see the other. Infographics shown in a report or on a shared link appear in landscape.

# After it is made
A finished infographic's look and contents cannot be changed. To change them, make a new one. Infographics made before the AI-designed pages keep their older space themes.

# Download
Click **Download** on the infographic and choose **Landscape**, **Portrait** or **Both**. You get a PNG with the Notestack logo and name in the corner. It takes a few seconds to prepare.

# Share
Click **Share** on the infographic to make a public link. See the sharing topic.

# Rate it
Under every finished infographic there are a thumbs up and a thumbs down. A thumbs down asks what was wrong (optional) so it can be improved. Click the same thumb again to take your rating back.

# Limits
Infographics are counted against your plan (the exact numbers are in the plans and pricing topic). Reaching the limit shows an upgrade prompt. Your usage is under **Plan and usage** in **Settings**.
