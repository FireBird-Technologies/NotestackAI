# What a report is
A report is a longer written piece made from the posts and chats you pick: a briefing, a study guide, a blog post or one you describe yourself. Every report ends with a Sources section listing the posts it used.

# Make a report
1. Open **Notebooks** and open a notebook. It needs at least one post.
2. In the **Create** panel on the right, click **Report**.
3. Under **Made from**, choose the posts and, if you like, chats. Everything in the notebook starts ticked. Pick at least one post or chat.
4. Choose a format: **Interactive** (a report with embedded visuals) or **Document** (structured text only).
5. For a Document, choose a template. Click **Next** to see and edit the instructions, or click **Generate** to use them as they are.
6. Click **Generate**. It runs in the background and appears in the Create panel and in the **Library** when it is ready.

# Templates
- **Create Your Own**: you write the instructions yourself.
- **Briefing Doc**: an overview of your sources with key insights and quotes.
- **Study Guide**: a short answer quiz with an answer key, essay questions and a glossary.
- **Blog Post**: the takeaways as a readable article.
- **Suggested Template**: after you pick sources, the AI writes a few templates that fit them. They appear under the fixed ones and may take a moment.
Click the pencil on a template to read and change its instructions before generating. The instructions go to the AI together with your sources.

# Interactive reports
An interactive report suggests where a visual would help. Open the report and a card reads **Suggested** under a section, for example a Mind Constellation, Flashcards, a Quiz, a comparison table, a timeline, key terms or an infographic. Nothing is made until you click **Add** on the card. For a Mind Constellation, quiz, flashcards or infographic you can make a new one or use one you already made. Under any added visual, **Remove this** takes it out. A Mind Constellation can only be added to a report made from posts, not from chats alone.

# Open, download, share, delete
- **Open report** on the report card opens the full page with a contents list that follows you down the page.
- **Download Markdown** is a button on the report page (not on the card) and saves the report as a Markdown file.
- **Share** makes a public link. See the sharing topic.
- **Delete** removes it.
# After it is made
The text of a finished report cannot be edited on the page. To change it, make a new report with different instructions. On an interactive report you can add suggested visuals and remove them. You cannot upload your own images. There is no language setting; you can ask for a language in the instructions, but Notestack does not promise the result.

# Limits
Reports are counted against your plan (the exact numbers are in the plans and pricing topic). When you reach the limit, clicking **Generate** shows an upgrade prompt and nothing is made. Your usage is in **Settings** under **Plan and usage**.
A report is made from up to 20 posts and 5 chats (the usual limit; it can be set differently on your site). Reports are made inside a notebook, so you need one with at least one post first.

# Rate it
A finished report has a thumbs up and a thumbs down at the top of its page and under its card. A thumbs down asks what was wrong (optional) so it can be improved. Click the same thumb again to take your rating back.
