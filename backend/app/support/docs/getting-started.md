# What Notestack is
Notestack turns a blog, newsletter, website or folder of markdown into a grounded research notebook. From that notebook you can make audio overviews, videos, reports, quizzes, flashcards, infographics, Mind Constellations and Launch Kits. Every answer in a notebook chat cites the exact lines you wrote, and anything it cannot verify from your writing is dropped.

# First steps
1. Open **Sources** and add your blog, newsletter or a single article link, or upload files.
2. Wait for the sync to finish. New posts from a connected feed arrive automatically.
3. Open **Notebooks**, create a notebook and choose the posts to put in it.
4. Ask your notebook a question, or use the **Create** panel to make audio, video, a report, a summary, an infographic, a quiz, flashcards or a Mind Constellation.
5. Use a Launch Kit and the Launchpad to turn posts into social content and schedule it.

# Mission Control
Mission Control is the home screen of the app. It shows a pre-flight checklist, jobs in flight, your sources and notebooks, recent launches and upcoming posts.

# The main menu
The sidebar has Notebooks, Sources, Library and Launchpad. Settings is at the bottom of the sidebar. **Library** holds everything you have made. Topic map, Voice profile, Studio, Launch Kit and Resurfacing are reachable from those pages.
