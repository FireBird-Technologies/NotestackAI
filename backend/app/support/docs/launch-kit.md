# What a Launch Kit is
A Launch Kit turns one of your posts into ready to publish social content, in your writing voice.

# What is inside
Hooks, an X thread, a LinkedIn post, Substack Notes, a Bluesky post, an SEO pack, a carousel and quote cards. Everything is editable before you use it.

# Make one
1. Open **Launch Kit** from the app.
2. Pick the post you want to promote.
3. Start the kit and wait for it to finish.
4. Edit any piece, then send it to the Launchpad to schedule it.

Launch Kits depend on your plan: Free has 2 in total (they do not renew), Writer 20 a month and Studio 50 a month.
