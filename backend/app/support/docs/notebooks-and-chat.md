# Create a notebook
1. Open **Notebooks**.
2. Click **New notebook**.
3. Choose the posts to include, or pick them by topic.
4. Click create. You can add or remove posts later from the notebook's post list.

# Ask your notebook
Open a notebook and type your question in the box at the bottom. The chats list is on the left, the chat is in the middle and the **Create** panel is on the right. You need at least one post in the notebook. Suggested questions appear on an empty chat.

# Citations
Answers carry numbered markers like [1]. Click one to open the exact lines from your post that support the claim. The sources under an answer list each post once with a number for every place it was cited. Notestack verifies citations before you see them. If something is not in your writing, the answer says so, and claims it cannot verify are dropped.

# Chat history and new chats
Chats are kept per notebook. Use the chat sidebar to switch between past conversations and the new chat button to start a fresh one. Under each answer you can **Copy answer** (the [1] markers are left out) and rate it with thumbs up or down. Click the notebook title to rename it.

# Make things from your notebook
The **Create** panel makes audio, video, reports, infographics, quizzes, flashcards and Mind Constellations. Quizzes, flashcards, reports and infographics can be made from chosen posts, chats, or both together.

# Writer notes
Standing notes about your audience, tone or things to avoid can be saved and are used in answers. Manage them in **Settings** under what the assistant should know.
