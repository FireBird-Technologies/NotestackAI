# Where to make things
Open a notebook. The **Create** panel on the right has **Mind Constellation**, **Audio overview**, **Video**, **Report**, **Infographic**, **Quiz** and **Flashcards**. Audio opens its options when clicked; the others open their own settings or start straight away.

# Audio overviews
Two hosts discuss your notebook, voiced on ElevenLabs voices. Choose a format (Deep dive, Brief or Debate) and a length in minutes, then click the main create button. With a consented voice clone on a paid plan, a clone can replace one host.

# Video
- 9:16 shorts for social feeds
- 16:9 explainers
- 1:1 audiograms, made from an audio overview
A video can be made from up to 5 posts, and from chats too.

# Other formats
Reports, infographics, quizzes, flashcards and Mind Constellations are made from the same **Create** panel. They have their own topics. Quote cards are not offered in the Create panel right now; Launch Kits can still include quote cards.

# Finding and managing results
Everything you generate is stored in the **Library** (left sidebar) with tabs, players, downloads, retry and delete. Generation runs in the background, so you can leave the page. Audio overviews and videos depend on your plan: Free has 1 audio overview (any length) and 1 video in total, which never renew; Writer has 60 minutes of audio and 10 videos a month; Studio has 240 minutes and 20 videos a month.
