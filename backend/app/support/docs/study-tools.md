# Limits
A report can use up to 20 posts and 5 chats. A quiz or a flashcard set can use any number of posts, and up to 5 chats. With more than 20 posts, a quiz or flashcard set is made from the key ideas already found in each post, shared across the main themes of your selection, and each question or card still links to the lines it came from. These are the usual limits and can be set differently on your site. A video can use up to 5 posts.

# Quiz
1. Open a notebook and, in the **Create** panel, click **Quiz**.
2. Under **Made from**, pick posts and/or chats (at least one).
3. Choose what the quiz should focus on: a suggested topic or your own **Custom topic**. Leave it empty to cover everything.
4. Set **Level of difficulty** (Easy, Medium or Hard) and the **Question types**: Multiple Choice, Fill in the Blank, Short Answer. The quiz opens with your current chat selected. There is no number-of-questions setting.
5. Click **Generate Quiz**.
When it is ready, click **Start quiz** on its card. It opens full screen. Use **Check answer** after each question, then **Next**, and **See results** at the end. Your score so far shows at the top right. The results screen gives a medal or a "Better luck next time", and **View results in depth** lists every question: click one to go back to it, then **Go to results**. A short answer is not graded for you: click **Show answer**, then answer **Did you get it?** with **Yes** or **Not quite**. **Retake** starts again.

# Flashcards
1. In the **Create** panel click **Flashcards**.
2. Pick the posts and/or chats, a focus, **Number of cards** and **Level of difficulty**.
3. Click **Generate Flashcards**.
Click **Study cards** on the card to open the deck. Tap the card or press Space to flip it. Use **Got it** or **Review again**, **Previous** and **Next** to move, and **Shuffle** to mix the deck.

# Rate it
Under every finished quiz or flashcard set there are a thumbs up and a thumbs down. A thumbs down asks what was wrong (optional) so it can be improved. Click the same thumb again to take your rating back.

# Mind Constellation
A Mind Constellation is a galaxy-style map of the ideas in your posts. Click **Mind Constellation** in the **Create** panel, choose the posts, and create it. It is listed under Mind Constellations in the panel. Drag to move around, click a star to read it and hold Ctrl or Cmd while scrolling to zoom. It is made from posts, not chats.

# Full screen study space
Quizzes and flashcards open in a full screen space. Press Escape or click **Minimise** to tuck it into a small pill and **Open** to bring it back. **Close** ends it.

# Where they are kept
All of these are kept in the **Library** under their own tabs: Quizzes, Flashcards, Summaries and Mind Constellations. Quizzes and flashcards are not limited by plan counts the way reports and infographics are.
