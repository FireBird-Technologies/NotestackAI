# Topic map
Every post is tagged with topics, and topics are merged into a constellation of what you write about. Topics that are rising or dormant are highlighted. You can make a notebook from any topic.

# Resurfacing
Resurfacing scores your old posts for how evergreen they are and suggests reshare angles. It also shows posts from this day in past years. Send one straight into a Launch Kit or onto the calendar.
