# Built for people who publish
Notestack starts from your own writing: it syncs your archive, answers from it with exact citations, and turns it into audio, video and social content. It is not a general purpose chatbot and it does not answer from the open web.

# Compared with a general research notebook
Notestack syncs a whole blog or newsletter automatically, cites exact line ranges, drops claims it cannot verify, includes publishing tools (Launch Kits, Launchpad, auto posting) and learns your writing voice. The full comparison is at /notebooklm-alternative.

# Not available today
- In app checkout is still being finished, so billing may be switched off.
- The main app is not yet optimised for mobile.
- Substack Notes cannot be posted automatically; it sends a reminder.
- Anything not described in this help is not something to promise. When unsure, offer to pass the question to the team.

# Roadmap items
Planned work includes finishing checkout, an inner galaxy view in the topic map, and exploring whole websites through llms.txt and sitemaps. There is no date for any of them.
