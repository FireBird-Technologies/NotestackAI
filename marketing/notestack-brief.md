# Notestack AI: content brief for Blog2Video and BlogHub

## One line

**Notestack** ([notestack.ai](https://notestack.ai)) is the ultimate NotebookLM alternative for writers, bloggers and newsletter authors: it turns everything you have published into a research notebook that answers from your own words, then helps you turn those words into audio, video and posts.

## Short description (about 50 words)

Notestack connects to your blog, newsletter or website and pulls your whole archive into orbit. Ask it anything and it answers from what you actually wrote, citing the exact lines. Then turn any post into a two host audio overview, short videos, quote cards and a ready to post Launch Kit, all in your own voice.

## Long description (about 150 words)

Most writers have years of ideas scattered across hundreds of posts, and almost none of it gets used again. Notestack fixes that. Paste the address of your Substack, Ghost, WordPress or Medium publication, any RSS feed, or any website (even ones without a feed), or upload markdown and PDF files. Notestack reads every post and keeps them in sync as you publish.

From there, your archive becomes a research notebook. Ask a question and get an answer built only from your writing, with citations to the exact lines, so nothing is made up. Explore a map of the topics you keep returning to and the ones that went quiet. Turn any post into a two host audio overview, a 9:16 short, a 16:9 explainer or an audiogram. Generate a Launch Kit with an X thread, LinkedIn post, Substack Note, Bluesky post, SEO pack and carousel, then schedule it all from one calendar.

## Key features

- **Connect any source:** Substack (full archive), Ghost, WordPress, Medium, any RSS feed, whole websites, single URLs, and markdown, text, HTML or PDF uploads.
- **Grounded research notebooks:** answers come only from your posts, with citations to the exact lines. If your archive does not cover a question, it says so.
- **Topic map:** a constellation of your ideas, showing which topics are rising and which have gone dormant.
- **Voice profile:** learns how you write, so every draft sounds like you. Optional consented voice cloning for audio.
- **Audio overviews:** two hosts talk through any notebook or post.
- **Video studio:** 9:16 shorts, 16:9 explainers, 1:1 audiograms and quote cards in your brand colors.
- **Launch Kit:** hooks, X thread, LinkedIn post, Substack Notes, Bluesky post, SEO pack and carousel from one post.
- **Launchpad:** a calendar that auto posts to X, LinkedIn and Bluesky, with email reminders for everything else.
- **Resurfacing:** finds evergreen posts worth sharing again.

## How it fits with Blog2Video, PDF2Video and BlogHub

All four are made by Firebird Technologies and work together:

- **[Notestack](https://notestack.ai):** research and repurpose your whole archive (the thinking layer).
- **[Blog2Video](https://blog2video.app):** turn a single blog post or URL into a video.
- **[PDF2Video](https://pdf2vid.com):** turn a PDF, paper or deck into a narrated video.
- **[BlogHub](https://bloghub.app):** a free directory where readers discover blogs and newsletters.

A natural flow: write and publish, list your publication on BlogHub so readers can find it, use Notestack to research and repurpose the archive, and use Blog2Video for standalone videos of single posts.

**Positioning rule:** Notestack is the NotebookLM alternative. BlogHub is a blog and newsletter directory, and Blog2Video and PDF2Video are video tools. Do not describe BlogHub, Blog2Video or PDF2Video as NotebookLM alternatives.

## Tone and style

- Warm, curious, a little space themed ("your knowledge, in orbit", "explore", "launch"), never hypey.
- Lead with the feeling (rediscovering your own ideas), then the proof (answers cite your exact lines).
- No em dashes. Use a colon, comma or a new sentence instead.
- Brand name: **Notestack** in text; "Notestack AI" is fine in headlines and video outros.
- Tagline: **Your knowledge, in orbit.**
